Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Omok Online ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Omok Online website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use Omok Online, you may provide a nickname to identify yourself during gameplay. This nickname is chosen by you and does not need to be your real name. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any other personal information to use the Service.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access the Service, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage, including:

  • Device information: Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, and interactions with the Service.
  • IP address: Your Internet Protocol address may be collected by our hosting provider and third-party services for analytics and security purposes.

1.3 Game Data

Game-related data, including room codes, move history, player nicknames, and game outcomes, is stored in our database (powered by Supabase) to enable real-time multiplayer functionality. This data is necessary for the core operation of the Service.

2. Local Storage

Omok Online uses your browser's localStorage to save your nickname and basic preferences locally on your device. This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or site data.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Omok Online itself does not set cookies for tracking purposes. However, our third-party partners, including advertising networks and analytics providers, may use cookies and similar technologies when you interact with the Service. These technologies are used to:

  • Deliver and measure the effectiveness of advertisements.
  • Understand user behavior and improve the Service.
  • Provide personalized advertising based on your interests.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, though doing so may affect your experience on some websites.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our Service. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide relevant advertisements. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by its own privacy policy. You can learn more about Google's data practices and opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4.2 Supabase

We use Supabase as our backend service for real-time game data storage and synchronization. Game data stored in Supabase includes room information, player nicknames, move history, and game states. Supabase processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policy and data protection practices.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the core gameplay functionality of the Service.
  • To enable real-time multiplayer game sessions between players.
  • To display advertisements that support the free operation of the Service.
  • To monitor and analyze usage trends to improve the Service.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

6. Data Retention

Game data (room information, move history, and associated nicknames) may be retained in our database for a limited period for operational purposes and is periodically cleaned up. We do not retain game data indefinitely. Local storage data persists on your device until you manually clear it.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information transmitted through our Service. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Omok Online is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not require account creation or personal information beyond a self-chosen nickname, the risk of inadvertent collection of children's data is minimal. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below, and we will take steps to remove such information.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data. Since we collect minimal personal data and do not require accounts, most data associated with your use of the Service can be managed directly through your browser (e.g., clearing local storage). For any data-related requests, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
